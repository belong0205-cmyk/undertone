# Food preparation equipment and knife skills — SITHCCC023 Use food preparation equipment

## Knives
| Knife | Use |
|---|---|
| Cook's / chef's knife (20–30 cm) | general chopping, slicing, dicing |
| Paring / vegetable knife (8–10 cm) | peeling, trimming, small detailed work, tourné (turning knife has a curved blade) |
| Boning knife (rigid, narrow) | removing bones from meat and poultry |
| Filleting knife (flexible) | filleting fish |
| Carving / slicing knife | slicing cooked meats |
| Serrated / bread knife | bread, cakes, tomatoes |
| Cleaver / chopper | chopping through bones |
| Palette knife | spreading, lifting, icing cakes |
| Sharpening steel | hones/realigns the edge between uses (does not remove metal) |
| Whetstone (sharpening stone) | sharpens by grinding a new edge (coarse then fine grit), used with water/oil |
| Turning knife, zester, channel knife, peeler, melon baller, mandoline | specialised cuts and garnishes |
- Knife safety: keep knives sharp (a blunt knife slips and needs more force); use the right knife; cut on a stable board (damp cloth underneath); claw grip (fingertips tucked, knuckles guide the blade); cut away from the body; never try to catch a falling knife; carry pointing down beside your leg, blade facing backwards; don't leave knives in sinks or on bench edges; store in knife rolls, magnetic racks or blocks; clean and sanitise immediately after use, especially between raw and cooked food.
- Honing angle on a steel approximately 15–20°.

## Classical cuts (sizes vary slightly between textbooks; these are the commonly taught ones)
| Cut | Description / size |
|---|---|
| Julienne | fine strips about 2 mm × 2 mm × 4–5 cm |
| Allumette | matchsticks about 3 mm × 3 mm × 5–6 cm (often for potatoes) |
| Batonnet | sticks about 5 mm × 5 mm × 5–6 cm |
| Jardinière | short batons about 4 mm × 4 mm × 2 cm |
| Brunoise | very small dice about 2 mm (cut from julienne); fine brunoise 1 mm |
| Macédoine | dice about 5 mm (cut from batonnet) |
| Mirepoix | rough dice of aromatic vegetables (onion, carrot, celery), size matched to cooking time (1–2 cm) |
| Paysanne | thin (1–2 mm) slices of evenly shaped pieces about 1 cm — squares, triangles, rounds |
| Parmentier | dice about 1–1.2 cm (often potatoes) |
| Chiffonade | fine ribbons of leafy greens/herbs (roll leaves and slice) |
| Concassé | tomatoes blanched, peeled, deseeded and diced |
| Tourné | barrel shape with 7 equal sides, about 5 cm long |
| Château / pont-neuf | potato shapes: château = large tourné; pont-neuf = thick chips about 2 cm × 2 cm × 6 cm |
| Rondelle | rounds/discs |
| Emincer | to slice thinly |
| Ciseler | finely dice (shallots/onions) / score fish |
Reasons for uniform cuts: even cooking, consistent presentation, portion control, professional standard.

## Small equipment
- Measuring: digital scales, measuring jugs/cups/spoons, probe thermometer, sugar (candy) thermometer, timer.
- Pans: saucepans, sauteuse (sloped sides), sauté pan (straight sides), frying pan, wok, stockpot, braising pan/cocotte, roasting tray, griddle pan, bain-marie pots, fish kettle, crêpe pan, omelette pan.
- Bakeware: cake tins, flan rings, tart tins, baking trays, silicone mats, piping bags and nozzles, rolling pins, pastry brushes, cutters, cooling racks, dariole/ramekin moulds.
- Utensils: whisks (balloon and sauce), spatulas (rubber, palette), ladles, spiders, skimmers, slotted spoons, tongs, fish slice, colander, chinois (fine conical strainer), conical strainer, sieve/drum sieve (tamis), mouli (food mill), muslin, mandoline, grater, mortar and pestle, scoops, portion-control ladles.
- Gastronorm (GN) containers: standard sizes (1/1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/6, 1/9) used in bain-maries, fridges, combi ovens.

## Using equipment safely and hygienically (assessment-style answer)
1. Select the right equipment for the task and quantity.
2. Check it is clean, assembled correctly, safe (cords, guards, blades) and working before use; report and tag faulty equipment "Do not use".
3. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and workplace procedures; only use equipment you are trained on.
4. Use safely: guards, pusher, PPE, never leave running unattended, switch off and unplug before cleaning or removing attachments.
5. After use: dismantle, clean, sanitise, dry, reassemble and store; report maintenance needs.
6. Minimise waste and energy use: preheat only when needed, switch off when not in use, full loads in dishwashers.
