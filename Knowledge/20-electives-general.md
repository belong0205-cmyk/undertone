# Group C electives — General

## SITXWHS006 Identify hazards, assess and control safety risks
Formal risk management: consult workers, identify hazards, assess with a risk matrix (likelihood × consequence), control with the hierarchy of control, record in a risk register, monitor and review. See file 02.

## HLTAID011 Provide First Aid
DRSABCD: Danger, Response (talk and touch), Send for help (000), Airway, Breathing (look, listen, feel 10 s), CPR (30 compressions : 2 breaths, 100–120/min, one-third of chest depth), Defibrillation (AED). Burns: cool running water 20 min, no ice or creams, cover loosely, medical help for large, deep, face/hands, chemical or electrical burns. Bleeding: direct pressure, elevate, bandage. Choking: cough; up to 5 back blows then 5 chest thrusts; 000. Anaphylaxis: adrenaline autoinjector (outer thigh), lay flat, 000, second dose after 5 min. Shock: lay down, keep warm, 000. Record and report incidents; keep kits stocked.

## BSBSUS211 Participate in sustainable work practices
Follow sustainability policies; reduce, reuse, recycle; save energy and water; buy local and seasonal; suggest improvements (see file 14).

## SITXCCS014 Provide service to customers
- Customer service: meeting or exceeding customer expectations with friendly, prompt, accurate and professional service.
- Verbal: words, tone, speed, clear language. Non-verbal: smile, eye contact, posture, gestures, facial expression, appearance.
- Active listening: eye contact and nodding, don't interrupt, repeat back or paraphrase, ask clarifying questions, take notes.
- Open questions: "What would you like to drink?", "How was your meal?", "What flavours do you enjoy?", "How would you like your steak cooked?", "What dietary needs should we know about?". Closed: "Do you have a booking?", "Would you like water?", "Is it a table for two?", "Any allergies?", "Would you like dessert?".
- Phone: answer within three rings with a greeting, the business name and your name; speak clearly and smile; note name, number, date, time, numbers and requests; ask before putting on hold and check back; tell the caller who you're transferring them to and pass on the details; confirm details, thank them, let them hang up first.
- Greeting: "Good evening, welcome to [restaurant]. Do you have a booking? Right this way… My name is [name], I'll be looking after you tonight."
- Upset about the wait: apologise sincerely, acknowledge it, explain briefly without blaming, give an honest time, check with the kitchen, offer something within your authority (bread, a drink), tell the supervisor, follow up.
- Presentation: clean pressed uniform, name badge, hair neat and tied back, short clean nails, minimal jewellery, good hygiene, no strong perfume, no phone.
- Confirm needs: repeat the order back, check allergies and dietary needs, cooking preferences and timing.
- Ethical upselling: suggest genuinely suitable extras (sides, drinks, desserts) honestly, no pressure, accept a no, never mislead (Australian Consumer Law).
- Complaints: LAST — Listen, Apologise, Solve, Thank; follow up; escalate to the supervisor when outside your authority.
- Allergies to the kitchen: table and customer, exact allergen and severity (anaphylaxis?), dishes and changes; "ALLERGY" on the docket and tell the chef directly; confirm before serving.
- Limited English: speak slowly and simply, no slang, gestures, pictures or menu photos, write numbers down, translation app or multilingual staff, check understanding, be patient.
- Front and back of house teamwork: pass on orders, timing, 86'd items, specials, allergies, feedback and complaints; briefings; respect.
- Records: complaint and feedback register, incident and allergen reports, refunds and comps, follow-up actions.

## SITXCOM007 Show social and cultural sensitivity
Respect diversity among customers and colleagues; no stereotypes or discrimination (anti-discrimination law, EEO); adapt communication (plain English, gestures, visuals, interpreters); respect religious food laws and holidays; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural awareness.

## SITXCOM006, SIRXOSM002, SIRXOSM003 Information and social media
Use reliable, current sources and present information clearly. Follow the social media policy: protect privacy, nothing offensive or misleading (Australian Consumer Law), reply to reviews professionally.
