# Coach others in job skills — SITXHRM007 (short notes)

- Coaching = on-the-job, one-to-one guidance from an experienced colleague on a practical skill (use the slicer, fillet a fish); for new staff, new equipment, menu items, procedures or poor performance.
- Process: identify the need → plan (objective, learner's current skills, quiet time, real work area, standard recipe/SOP, WHS and food safety) → explain purpose, standard and safety → demonstrate at normal speed then slowly → learner practises with guidance then alone → specific, timely, balanced feedback → follow up and record. Shorthand: Tell → Show → Do → Review.
- Adults learn best when it is relevant, practical and respectful, with practice and feedback; plain language, open questions, patience. Not progressing: find the cause, smaller steps, more practice, tell the supervisor; unsafe → stop at once.
- Records: date, skill, learner, outcome — evidence of food handler training (Std 3.2.2A).
