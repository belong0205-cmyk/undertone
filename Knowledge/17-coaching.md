# Coach others in job skills — SITXHRM007 (short notes)

- Coaching is on-the-job, one-to-one guidance from an experienced colleague. It covers a practical skill, like using the slicer. It is for new staff, new equipment, menu items, procedures or poor performance.
- Process: I identify the need. I plan the objective, the learner's current skills and a quiet time. I use the real work area and cover WHS and food safety. I explain the purpose, the standard and the safety points. I demonstrate at normal speed, then slowly. The learner practises with guidance, then alone. I give specific, timely feedback. I follow up and record it: date, skill, learner, outcome (training evidence, Std 3.2.2A). Shorthand: Tell → Show → Do → Review. If they are not progressing, I find the cause. I use smaller steps and more practice, and tell the supervisor. If it is unsafe, I stop at once.
