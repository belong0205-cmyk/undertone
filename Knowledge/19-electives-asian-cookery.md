# Group B electives — Asian Cookery (SITHASC020–028, SITHASC033)

## Basic methods of Asian cookery (SITHASC020)
Stir-frying (wok hei — "breath of the wok": very high heat, small uniform pieces, cook in batches, prepare all ingredients first), deep frying (tempura, spring rolls, karaage), steaming (bamboo steamers — dim sum, whole fish, buns), braising and red-cooking (soy-braised meats), simmering (soups, curries), roasting (char siu, Peking duck), grilling/barbecuing (satay, yakitori, bulgogi), poaching (Hainanese chicken), smoking (tea-smoked duck), boiling (noodles, dumplings), tandoor (clay oven), velveting (marinating meat in egg white and cornflour then blanching in oil/water for a silky texture), dry-frying/tempering spices (tadka — blooming whole spices in hot oil/ghee).
Equipment: wok and wok chuan (spatula), spider/strainer, cleaver, bamboo steamer, rice cooker, mortar and pestle, clay pot, tandoor, sushi mat (makisu), tawa (flat griddle), mandoline, spice grinder.

## Asian appetisers and snacks (SITHASC021)
Spring rolls (fried), fresh rice paper rolls, dim sum (har gow, siu mai), gyoza/potstickers, wontons, samosas, pakoras/bhajis, satay skewers, prawn toast, money bags, curry puffs, edamame, takoyaki, tempura, sushi rolls, chicken karaage, fish cakes (Thai tod mun pla), pani puri, bánh xèo (Vietnamese crispy pancakes), lettuce cups (san choy bow). Served with dipping sauces (nước chấm, sweet chilli, soy-vinegar, tamarind, raita, peanut sauce).

## Asian stocks and soups (SITHASC022)
Dashi (kombu + katsuobushi, infused not boiled), Chinese superior/chicken stock (chicken, pork bones, ginger, spring onion), pho broth (charred onion and ginger, star anise, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom, coriander seed, beef bones, simmered long and clear), tonkotsu ramen broth (pork bones boiled hard for a milky emulsion), tom yum (lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime, chilli, fish sauce, lime), tom kha (with coconut milk), laksa (curry laksa with coconut; assam laksa sour tamarind fish broth), miso soup (add miso off the boil), hot and sour soup, wonton soup, congee (rice porridge), mulligatawny, rasam.

## Asian sauces, dips and accompaniments (SITHASC023)
Nước chấm (fish sauce, lime, sugar, water, garlic, chilli), nam jim (Thai dressing), sweet chilli, satay/peanut sauce, hoisin, black bean sauce, sweet and sour, XO sauce, teriyaki (soy, mirin, sake, sugar), ponzu (soy + citrus), tare, sambal oelek, sambal belacan, chilli oil, raita (yoghurt, cucumber, mint), mint and coriander chutney, tamarind chutney, mango chutney, pickles (achar, kimchi, pickled ginger, Vietnamese đồ chua — pickled carrot and daikon), crispy fried shallots/garlic, toasted peanuts/sesame, sambal, papadums.

## Asian salads (SITHASC024)
Som tam (green papaya — pounded in mortar), larb (minced meat with lime, fish sauce, roasted rice powder, herbs), yum woon sen (glass noodle salad), Vietnamese chicken salad (gỏi gà), Thai beef salad (yum nua), seaweed salad, gado gado (vegetables with peanut sauce), raita/kachumber, Japanese sunomono (cucumber vinegar salad). Balance flavours, use fresh herbs, dress at the last minute, add crunch (peanuts, crispy shallots).

## Asian rice and noodles (SITHASC025)
- Rice: jasmine (Thai, fragrant long grain — absorption method), basmati (Indian — rinse, soak, boil or pilaf/biryani), short-grain Japanese/sushi rice (rinse until water runs clear, cook by absorption, season with sushi vinegar — rice vinegar, sugar, salt), glutinous/sticky rice (soaked then steamed — mango sticky rice), brown rice; fried rice from cold cooked rice; congee; biryani (layered rice and curry, dum cooking — sealed); nasi lemak (coconut rice).
- Noodles: wheat (egg noodles, hokkien, ramen, udon, somen, la mian), rice (vermicelli, rice sticks — pho, pad Thai, laksa, flat ho fun — char kway teow), buckwheat (soba), mung bean/glass (cellophane) noodles, sweet potato noodles (japchae). Soak or boil per type, refresh to stop cooking, toss with oil to prevent sticking.
- Rice safety: Bacillus cereus — cool rice rapidly, refrigerate ≤5°C, hold ≥60°C, don't hold at room temperature.

## Curry pastes and powders (SITHASC026)
- Thai green curry paste: green chillies, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallots, coriander root, kaffir lime zest, shrimp paste, cumin, coriander seed, white pepper. Red: dried red chillies instead. Massaman: dry spices (cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, nutmeg) with roasted shallots/garlic — Persian/Indian influence. Panang: red curry style with peanuts.
- Malaysian rempah (spice paste for rendang/laksa): shallots, garlic, chilli, galangal, lemongrass, turmeric, candlenuts, belacan.
- Indian masalas: garam masala (warming spices — added at the end), curry powder, tandoori masala, vindaloo paste (chilli, vinegar, garlic), korma (nuts, cream, mild spices), Madras (hot).
- Technique: toast whole spices in a dry pan until fragrant, cool and grind; pound fresh aromatics in mortar and pestle or blend with a little oil; fry the paste in oil/coconut cream until fragrant and oil separates ("split") before adding liquid; store paste covered in the fridge (short term) or frozen; store ground spices airtight away from light and heat.

## Asian cooked dishes (SITHASC027)
Curries (Thai green chicken, beef rendang — dry curry cooked down until coconut caramelises, butter chicken, lamb rogan josh, dhal), stir-fries (beef in black bean sauce, kung pao chicken, pad krapow — holy basil), braised dishes (red-braised pork belly, Vietnamese caramel pork/fish kho), steamed whole fish with ginger and spring onion, Hainanese chicken rice, tandoori chicken, teriyaki salmon, tonkatsu, bulgogi, Peking duck, sweet and sour pork, mapo tofu, nasi goreng, char kway teow, pad Thai (rice noodles, tamarind, fish sauce, palm sugar, egg, tofu, peanuts, bean sprouts).

## Asian desserts (SITHASC028)
Mango sticky rice, black sticky rice pudding, sago/tapioca pudding with coconut and palm sugar (gula melaka), red bean soup, mochi, matcha ice cream, dorayaki, egg tarts (dan tat), sesame balls (jian dui), almond jelly, che (Vietnamese sweet soups), halo-halo, kulfi (Indian ice cream), gulab jamun (fried milk dumplings in syrup), mango lassi, chendol, pandan cake, kuih, banana fritters.

## Dim sum (SITHASC033)
- Meaning "touch the heart"; served with tea (yum cha). Types: steamed (har gow — prawn dumplings in translucent wheat starch dough; siu mai — open-topped pork and prawn dumplings in wonton wrappers; char siu bao — steamed BBQ pork buns made with leavened dough; cheung fun — rice noodle rolls; xiao long bao — soup dumplings with gelatinous stock), fried (spring rolls, wu gok — taro puffs, prawn toast, sesame balls), baked (char siu sou, egg tarts), pan-fried (potstickers, turnip/radish cake — lo bak go), sweet (mango pudding, custard buns).
- Doughs/wrappers: wheat starch + tapioca starch with boiling water (har gow — translucent), wheat flour wonton/gow gee wrappers, yeasted bao dough, rice flour batter (cheung fun), glutinous rice dough (sesame balls).
- Technique: fill and pleat neatly with consistent size and number of pleats, seal well to prevent bursting, steam on lined baskets (paper/cabbage leaf) over rapidly boiling water for the correct time, serve immediately with condiments (soy, chilli oil, vinegar, ginger).
- Food safety: high-risk fillings (pork, prawn) — keep chilled while folding in small batches; cook to safe core temperature; cool and freeze any made ahead rapidly.

## Cultural knowledge
Respect authenticity, ingredients and naming; understand religious requirements (halal for many Malaysian/Indonesian customers, vegetarian Indian dishes), common allergens in Asian cooking (peanuts, sesame, soy, shellfish, fish sauce, gluten in soy sauce and noodles, MSG sensitivity).
