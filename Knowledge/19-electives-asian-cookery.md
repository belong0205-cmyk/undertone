## Methods and equipment (SITHASC020)
Stir-fry (very hot wok, small even pieces, cook in batches, everything ready first), deep fry, steam (bamboo baskets), braise and red-cook, simmer, roast (char siu), grill (satay, yakitori), poach (Hainanese chicken), tandoor, velveting (egg white + cornflour marinade, blanched in oil or water — silky), tempering spices in hot oil or ghee (tadka). Equipment: wok, cleaver, bamboo steamer, rice cooker, mortar and pestle, clay pot, tawa, sushi mat.

## Dishes by unit (SITHASC021–028)
- Appetisers: spring rolls, rice paper rolls, dumplings (gyoza, wontons, har gow, siu mai), samosas, pakoras, satay, tempura, karaage, Thai fish cakes, san choy bow; with dipping sauces.
- Stocks and soups: dashi (infused, not boiled), Chinese chicken stock (chicken, pork bones, ginger, spring onion), pho broth (charred onion and ginger, star anise, cinnamon, cloves, beef bones, long clear simmer), tonkotsu (pork bones boiled hard — milky), tom yum, tom kha (coconut), laksa, miso soup (miso added off the boil), congee.
- Sauces and accompaniments: nuoc cham (fish sauce, lime, sugar, water, garlic, chilli), nam jim, sweet chilli, peanut, hoisin, black bean, teriyaki (soy, mirin, sake, sugar), ponzu, sambal, chilli oil, raita, chutneys, pickles (kimchi, pickled ginger, carrot and daikon), crispy shallots.
- Salads: som tam (pounded green papaya), larb (mince, lime, fish sauce, roasted rice powder, herbs), Thai beef, Vietnamese chicken, glass noodle; balance flavours, add crunch, dress at the last minute.
- Rice: jasmine (absorption), basmati (rinse, soak), sushi rice (rinse until clear, season with rice vinegar, sugar, salt), glutinous (soak, steam), fried rice from cold cooked rice. Noodles: wheat (egg, hokkien, ramen, udon), rice (vermicelli, flat), soba, glass; soak or boil per type, refresh, oil lightly. Bacillus cereus — cool rice fast, keep ≤5°C or ≥60°C.
- Curry pastes (SITHASC026): Thai green — green chilli, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallot, coriander root, kaffir lime zest, shrimp paste, cumin, coriander seed (red: dried red chilli); Malaysian rempah — shallot, garlic, chilli, galangal, lemongrass, turmeric, candlenut, belacan; Indian — garam masala (added at the end), tandoori masala, vindaloo (chilli, vinegar, garlic), korma (nuts, cream). Toast and grind whole spices; pound or blend aromatics; fry the paste until fragrant and the oil separates; store paste chilled or frozen, spices airtight.
- Cooked dishes: green curry, beef rendang (cooked down until the coconut caramelises), butter chicken, rogan josh, dhal, kung pao chicken, red-braised pork belly, Vietnamese caramel pork or fish, steamed whole fish with ginger and spring onion, Hainanese chicken rice, teriyaki salmon, bulgogi, pad Thai.
- Desserts: mango sticky rice, black sticky rice, sago with coconut and palm sugar, mochi, egg tarts, sesame balls, Vietnamese che, kulfi, gulab jamun, pandan cake, banana fritters.

## Dim sum (SITHASC033)
Served with tea (yum cha). Steamed: har gow, siu mai, char siu bao, rice noodle rolls, xiao long bao; fried: spring rolls, taro puffs; baked: egg tarts; pan-fried: potstickers, turnip cake. Even pleats, seal well, steam on lined baskets over rapidly boiling water, serve at once. Pork and prawn fillings are high risk — keep chilled, fold in small batches, cook through.

## Cultural and allergen notes
Respect authentic names and methods; halal for many Malaysian and Indonesian customers; many Indian dishes are vegetarian. Allergens: peanuts, sesame, soy, shellfish, fish sauce, gluten in soy sauce and wheat noodles.
