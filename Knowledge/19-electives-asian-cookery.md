# Group B electives — Asian Cookery (SITHASC020–028, SITHASC033) — short notes (not in the SA Cook questionnaire)

- Flavours: Chinese — soy, oyster sauce, ginger, five-spice; Japanese — dashi, soy, mirin, miso; Thai — fish sauce, palm sugar, lime, chilli, lemongrass, galangal, coconut milk (hot, sour, salty, sweet in balance); Vietnamese — fresh herbs, fish sauce, rice noodles; Malaysian/Indonesian — sambal, belacan, kecap manis; Indian — cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, ghee.
- Methods: stir-fry (very hot wok, small even pieces, in batches, everything ready first), steam, deep fry, braise, grill (satay), velveting (egg white and cornflour), tempering spices in hot oil. Dashi is infused, not boiled; pho broth simmers long and clear. Curry pastes: toast and grind spices, pound aromatics, fry until the oil separates. Dim sum steamed over rapidly boiling water; pork and prawn fillings kept chilled and cooked through. Cooked rice: cool fast, ≤5°C or ≥60°C (Bacillus cereus).
- Allergens: peanuts, sesame, soy, shellfish, fish sauce, gluten in soy sauce and wheat noodles; halal matters for many customers.
