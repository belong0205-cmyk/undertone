# Group B electives — Asian Cookery (SITHASC020–028, SITHASC033): notes, not in the SA Cook questionnaire

- Flavours: Chinese — soy, oyster sauce, ginger, five-spice. Japanese — dashi, soy, mirin, miso. Thai — fish sauce, palm sugar, lime, chilli, lemongrass, galangal, coconut milk. Thai food balances hot, sour, salty and sweet. Vietnamese — fresh herbs, fish sauce, rice noodles. Malaysian/Indonesian — sambal, belacan, kecap manis. Indian — cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, ghee.
- Methods: stir-fry in a very hot wok, small even pieces, in batches. Everything is ready first. Steam, deep fry, braise, grill (satay). Velveting (egg white and cornflour), tempering spices in hot oil. Dashi is infused, not boiled. Pho broth simmers long and stays clear. Curry pastes: I toast and grind spices, then pound aromatics. I fry the paste until the oil separates. Dim sum is steamed over fast-boiling water. Pork and prawn fillings stay chilled and are cooked through. Cooked rice: cool fast, ≤5°C or ≥60°C (Bacillus cereus).
- Allergens: peanuts, sesame, soy, shellfish, fish sauce, gluten in soy sauce and wheat noodles. Halal matters for many customers.
