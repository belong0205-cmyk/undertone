# Group B electives — Asian Cookery (SITHASC020–028, SITHASC033). Not in the SA Cook questionnaire.

- Flavours: Chinese — soy, oyster sauce, ginger, five-spice. Japanese — dashi, soy, mirin, miso. Thai — fish sauce, palm sugar, lime, chilli, lemongrass, galangal. Thai food balances hot, sour, salty and sweet. Vietnamese — fresh herbs, fish sauce, rice noodles. Malaysian/Indonesian — sambal, belacan, kecap manis. Indian — cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala.
- Methods: stir-fry in a very hot wok, small even pieces, in batches. Steam, deep fry, braise, grill (satay). Curry pastes: I toast and grind spices, then pound aromatics. I fry the paste until the oil separates. Dim sum fillings (pork, prawn) stay chilled and are cooked through. Cooked rice: cool fast, ≤5°C or ≥60°C (Bacillus cereus).
- Allergens: peanuts, sesame, soy, shellfish, fish sauce, gluten in soy sauce and wheat noodles. Halal matters for many customers.
